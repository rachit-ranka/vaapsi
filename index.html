The situation

Two kinds of land, one kind of problem.

Whether the land has been in your family for generations, or you're looking to put money into a farm for the first time — not being there is the obstacle. Vaapsi exists to remove it.

Ancestral land

"My family's land back in the village has sat idle for years. My relatives mean well, but they don't have the time or the know-how to make it work."

You inherited it. You moved to the city. It deserves better than guilt and good intentions.
New investment

"I want to put money into farmland near Indore or Bhopal, but I've never run a farm in my life — and I don't want to learn the hard way."

You have the capital. You don't have the time, the land, or the experience — yet.
How vaapsi works

From idle land to a living farm — and back to you.

A simple loop, repeated every season: we listen, we plan, we farm, you receive. No part of it requires you to be on-site.

1

Tell us about your land

Where it is, how much there is, and what you're hoping for — income, legacy, or both.

2

We design the right model

Orchard, hydroponics, organic, permaculture — matched to your land, climate, and goals.

3

We build and farm it

Our team sets up and runs the farm on the ground, season after season.

4

It comes back to you

Reports, harvests, and returns — wherever in the world you are.

Farming approaches

Different land calls for different methods.

We don't force every plot into the same model. We assess your land and recommend the approach suited to it.

Hydroponics

Soil-free, water-efficient growing for high-value crops — ideal for capital-backed plots near urban demand.

Orchards

Mango, sandalwood, citrus and more — a long-horizon, low-intervention asset that grows with your family's legacy.

Polyhouse farming

Climate-controlled structures that protect premium vegetables and flowers from weather risk year-round.

Controlled farming

Data-driven, sensor-managed cultivation for consistent yield and quality — built for serious ROI.

Permaculture

Self-sustaining, low-cost systems that restore soil health and work in harmony with the land long-term.

Organic farming

Chemical-free cultivation with certification support — easier to market, and kinder to the land.
